# AI Policy

`MASTER_RULES.md` is authoritative.

AI providers are replaceable reasoning workers, not technical sources of truth. AI may normalize intent, retrieve approved knowledge, organize verified operations, propose dependency-safe ordering, explain evidence, and generate documentation.

AI must not invent vendor syntax, parameters, defaults, features, dependencies, version behavior, device facts, safety claims, or write authorization. Vendor commands must originate from deterministic vendor renderers or validated vendor knowledge.

For MikroTik, AI ordering is advisory only. Raw RouterOS CLI must remain owned by deterministic renderers and validators. Model output must pass dependency, conflict, semantic, version, dry-run, approval, and execution gates.
