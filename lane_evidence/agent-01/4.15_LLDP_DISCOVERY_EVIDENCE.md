## Normalized boundaries

- LLDP is a Layer-2 discovery mechanism for exchanging system/configuration information between neighboring devices.
- LLDP-MED is an LLDP extension oriented to endpoint/VoIP deployment and network-policy information.
- LLDP global state, per-port TX/RX/admin state, LLDP-MED TLVs/network policy, local information, neighbor information, management address, and timers must remain separate state planes.
- Controller objects must not be blindly translated into standalone CLI without exact model/firmware/runtime support.
- Exact model, hardware revision, firmware, Controller version, port/LAG role, and supported TLVs remain execution gates.
