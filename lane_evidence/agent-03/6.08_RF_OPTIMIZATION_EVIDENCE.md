## Safety boundaries

- Optimization does not mean "maximum transmit power". Excessive power can increase co-channel contention and asymmetric client behavior.
- Channel and width choices depend on country/region regulatory domain, exact model, radio capability, DFS support, neighboring RF conditions, and Controller/firmware version.
- 2.4 GHz and 5/6 GHz optimization must stay separate.
- A recommendation generated from stale scan/interference data is not a verified change.
